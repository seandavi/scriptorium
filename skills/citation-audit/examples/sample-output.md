## Patterns

### Unsupported assertions

- The opening sentence's reference to "a growing literature" carries
  no citation. Acceptable for true background-context throwaway
  phrasing; load-bearing here because it frames the discussion.
- "Established role of TMAO as a primary driver of atherogenesis"
  treats a mechanism as canonical without any citation.

### Causal overreach

- "*B. fragilis* causes plaque regression through TMAO suppression"
  — the only primary source (chen2023) reports correlation in mice
  and explicitly says causal inference requires further work. The
  citing sentence asserts both causation and a specific mechanism.

### Review-only support for mechanistic claims

- The mechanistic claim above is supported only by kumar2022review,
  a narrative review. For a discussion-section inference of this
  weight, the primary sources behind the review should be cited
  directly.

### Single-source load-bearing claims

- (none beyond the patterns above)

### Possible amplification / invention

- The chen2023 → kumar2022review → manuscript chain has the shape of
  Greenberg's *invention* distortion: a hedged primary finding
  ("correlation; causation needs further work") becomes a stated
  mechanism through citation of a narrative review.

## What this skill did NOT check

- Whether the full text of kumar2022review (which we did not
  receive) further qualifies the TMAO mechanism. Assessment is from
  the abstract only.
- Whether the chen2023 high-fat-diet caveat — which the manuscript's
  Limitations section does acknowledge — fully addresses the
  generalisability concern.
- Whether the bibliography itself contains errors (DOI accuracy,
  author-list completeness, etc.).
- Whether any of the cited papers have been retracted.
- Whether a better-supporting primary citation exists somewhere in
  the literature for the TMAO mechanistic claim. Suggesting
  alternative citations is out of scope.
