The Concepts section is the largest part of these docs — and deliberately so. The knowledge layer is the project's actual moat, and most of `knowledge/` lives naturally here.

## Start here

- [Design](/concepts/design/) — the agentic-scriptorium thesis, design philosophy, and the shared-state contract.
- [Knowledge layer](/concepts/knowledge/) — the evidence base every skill grounds in. Browse by subsection from the sidebar.

## What "Concepts" means here

Following the [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) framework, Concepts pages are *understanding-oriented* — they answer "why is it like this?" rather than "how do I do X?" (How-to guides) or "what does X do?" (Reference). When a design choice surprises you or you want to understand the lineage behind a decision, this is the section to read.
