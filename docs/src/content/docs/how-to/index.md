How-to guides are *task-oriented* — they assume you know what you're
trying to accomplish and just need the recipe. They are not a learning
path; the [Concepts](/concepts/design/) section is where the *why*
lives, and the [worked case study](/concepts/case-study/) is the
closest thing to an end-to-end walkthrough.

## Shipped

- [Install](/how-to/install/) — set scriptorium up for Claude Code, another agent, or manual use.
- [Convert your manuscript to markdown](/how-to/convert-to-markdown/) — for `.docx`, LaTeX, Google Docs, and the awkward cases.

## Future how-to ideas

recipe rather than a tutorial — the choice is deliberate. If you have
a real use case for one of these, an issue or PR is welcome.
