## Why dual-licensed

MIT is the convention for code; CC BY is the convention for prose. They compose cleanly: a downstream project may reuse skills (MIT) and quote knowledge-document framings (CC BY with attribution) without ambiguity over which license applies to which artifact.

The full text of both licenses ships in the repo: [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/seandavi/scriptorium/blob/main/LICENSE) (MIT) and [`LICENSE-DOCS`](https://github.com/seandavi/scriptorium/blob/main/LICENSE-DOCS) (CC BY 4.0 pointer + attribution guidance).
